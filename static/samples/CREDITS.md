# Sample images

`IDRiD_067.jpg`, `IDRiD_115.jpg`, `IDRiD_213.jpg`, `IDRiD_343.jpg` and their `_thumb` versions are from the
**Indian Diabetic Retinopathy Image Dataset (IDRiD)**, disease grading training set.

- Authors: Prasanna Porwal, Samiksha Pachade, Ravi Kamble, Manesh Kokare, Girish Deshmukh, Vivek Sahasrabuddhe, Fabrice Meriaudeau
- Source: IEEE Dataport, https://ieee-dataport.org/node/956, DOI [10.21227/H25W98](https://doi.org/10.21227/H25W98)
- Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (© Prasanna Porwal, Samiksha Pachade and Manesh Kokare)
- Changes: resized from 4288 × 2848 to 1280 × 850 (and 144 × 96 thumbnails) and re-encoded as JPEG.

The grades in `samples.json` are the dataset's expert labels. The model was trained only on APTOS 2019 and never saw IDRiD.
